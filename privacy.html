Privacy Policy

NIGH Backup - last updated 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

NIGH Backup is a private tool of New India Glass House ("NIGH"), Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. It is used only by the owners of NIGH, to keep backup copies of NIGH's own business data in NIGH's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public. More about the app is on the home page.

2. What Google data the app accesses

The app asks for ONE Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (Google Drive, per-file access). With this permission the app can see and manage only the files and folders that the app itself has created in the Google Drive of the account that signed in. It cannot see any other file or folder in that Google Drive. The app does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google service, and it does not collect information about any other person.

3. How the data is used

The app uses this access for one purpose only: to upload backup files from the private business server of NIGH into a folder named "nigh-backup" in the owners' Google Drive, to check that the upload is complete, and to delete the old backup files that the app itself created, when they are no longer needed under NIGH's backup schedule (for example, keeping the recent daily, weekly and monthly copies).

4. How the data is stored and protected

The backup files are stored only in the owners' own Google Drive (under Google's own security) and on NIGH's own private server. The files and their names are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded. The sign-in code of the app is stored only on NIGH's private server, in a file that only the administrator of that server can read.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. No person other than the owners of NIGH can see it through this app.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

NIGH Backup's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Backup files stay in the Google Drive until the app deletes them under the backup schedule, or until the owners delete them. The owners can delete them in Google Drive at any time. The owners can also stop the access of the app at any time in their Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions), and can delete the Google Cloud project of the app.

8. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information about children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be put on this page with a new date.
