NIGH Backup

A private backup tool of New India Glass House, Indore, India

What the app does

NIGH Backup is a small program that runs on the private business server of New India Glass House ("NIGH"), a glass business in Indore, India. Every night it copies the backup files of NIGH's own business system into a folder in the Google Drive of NIGH's owners, so that a safe copy of the company's data is kept outside the server. The files are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded.

Who uses it

Only the owners of NIGH. The app is not offered to the public, and nobody else is asked to sign in to it.

What it uses from Google

The app asks for one Google permission: Google Drive, only for the files and folders that the app itself creates (the scope drive.file). It cannot see, open, change or delete any other file in the Google Drive. It does not use Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google service.
